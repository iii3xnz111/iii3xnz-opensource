- host or manage iii3xnz for other people or companies (SaaS, managed hosting),
- resell or white-label iii3xnz,
- sell access to workflows or execution capacity (including agency or service-bureau setups that run on your infrastructure for many clients),
- embed iii3xnz in a product you sell that competes with it,
- use the paid/cloud functionality covered by the [Enterprise License](LICENSE_EE.md) in production under a subscription.

Building workflows for a client that run on the client's own self-hosted instance does not need a commercial license.
